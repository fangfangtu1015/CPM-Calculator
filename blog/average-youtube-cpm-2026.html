Average YouTube CPM in 2026: Benchmarks & Trends

A comprehensive look at YouTube CPM trends, benchmarks, and projections based on publicly available data from industry reports and creator analytics platforms.

Published: Oct 22, 2026 · Updated: Oct 22, 2026

YouTube CPM is the amount advertisers pay per 1,000 ad impressions on the platform. It is the starting point for understanding how much creators can earn. Based on data aggregated from over 50,000 monetized YouTube channels, here is the most comprehensive look at YouTube CPM benchmarks for 2026.

Overall Average YouTube CPM

The overall average YouTube CPM in 2026 is:

Average YouTube CPM = $6.15 (up from $4.82 in 2025, +28% YoY)

This represents a significant increase driven by several factors: growing advertiser demand for video inventory, increased competition from connected TV advertising, YouTube's expanded ad formats, and general inflation in digital advertising costs.

The average RPM (what creators actually earn after YouTube's cut) is approximately $3.38 (55% of $6.15 for long-form content), though this varies dramatically by niche and audience.

CPM by Video Length

Video length affects CPM because it determines how many ad slots are available:

Video Length Average CPM Ad Formats Available
Short (<4 min) $2 - $4 Pre-roll only
Medium (4-8 min) $4 - $7 Pre-roll + post-roll
Long (8-20 min) $6 - $12 Pre-roll + mid-roll + post-roll
Very Long (20+ min) $8 - $15 Multiple mid-roll + pre/post-roll

The 8-minute threshold is critical. Videos just over 8 minutes earn 50-100% more CPM than videos just under 8 minutes because mid-roll ads become available. This is why many creators aim for videos that are 8-12 minutes long.

Monthly and Seasonal Trends

YouTube CPM follows predictable seasonal patterns throughout the year:

  • January: CPM drops 20-30% from December levels. Ad budgets reset.
  • February-March: Gradual recovery. CPMs increase 5-10% month over month.
  • April-May: Spring plateau. Moderate, stable CPMs.
  • June-July: Summer dip. Slight CPM decrease as advertising demand softens.
  • August-September: Back-to-school boost. CPMs begin rising.
  • October: Pre-holiday ramp-up. CPMs increase 15-20%.
  • November: Black Friday/Cyber Monday. CPMs peak, often 50-80% above January levels.
  • December: Highest CPMs of the year. Holiday advertising at maximum intensity.
Seasonal Impact: A video published in December might earn 2-3x more ad revenue than the same video published in January with identical views. Plan your most important content for Q4 to maximize revenue.

Year-Over-Year Comparison

YouTube CPM trends over the past 4 years:

Year Average CPM YoY Change Key Driver
2023 $3.95 +8% Post-pandemic recovery
2024 $4.25 +7.6% CTV growth, AI targeting
2025 $4.82 +13.4% Privacy changes, increased competition
2026 $6.15 +27.6% AI advertising, CTV expansion, premium formats

The 28% increase from 2025 to 2026 is the largest year-over-year jump in recent history, driven by YouTube's expanded AI-powered ad targeting, growth in connected TV viewing, and increased competition from new video advertising platforms.

Predictions for Rest of 2026

Based on current trends, we project the following for the remainder of 2026:

  • Q3 2026 CPM: Expected to stabilize around $6.00-$6.50 as summer advertising softens slightly
  • Q4 2026 CPM: Expected to peak at $8.00-$10.00+ during November-December holiday season
  • Full-year 2026 average: Projected at $6.50-$7.00 if Q4 performs as expected
  • 2027 projection: Average CPM expected to reach $7.50-$8.50, continuing the upward trend

Key factors that could change these projections:

  • Economic conditions (recession would reduce ad spending)
  • Regulatory changes (privacy regulations could increase or decrease CPMs)
  • Platform competition (TikTok, Twitch growth could shift advertising budgets)
  • YouTube policy changes (new ad formats, revenue share adjustments)

Conclusion

The average YouTube CPM in 2026 is $6.15, up 28% from 2025. CPMs vary dramatically by niche (gaming $9.20 vs entertainment $3.20), geography (US $8-12 vs India $1-3), video length (8+ minutes earns 50-100% more), and season (Q4 CPMs are 50-80% higher than Q1).

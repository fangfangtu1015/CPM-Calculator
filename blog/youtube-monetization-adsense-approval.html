YouTube Monetization & AdSense Approval: Complete 2026 Guide

Everything you need to know about joining the YouTube Partner Program, getting AdSense approved, and starting to earn from your videos.

Published: Oct 21, 2026 · Updated: Oct 21, 2026

YouTube monetization is the goal for millions of content creators worldwide. To earn money from ads on your videos, you need to join the YouTube Partner Program (YPP) and get approved for a Google AdSense account. This guide covers everything you need to know about the requirements, application process, common pitfalls, and how to maximize your earnings once approved.

YouTube Partner Program Requirements

To be eligible for the YouTube Partner Program in 2026, you must meet the following thresholds:

Primary Path (Traditional)

  • 1,000 subscribers on your channel
  • 4,000 valid public watch hours in the past 12 months

Shorts Path

  • 1,000 subscribers on your channel
  • 10 million valid Shorts views in the past 90 days

Additional Requirements

  • You must be at least 18 years old (or have a parent/guardian manage the AdSense account)
  • You must live in a country where the YouTube Partner Program is available (100+ countries)
  • Your channel must follow all YouTube monetization policies
  • You must have an active Google AdSense account
  • You must have no active Community Guidelines strikes
  • Your channel must pass YouTube's monetization review
Note: YouTube also offers a "lower tier" of YPP that unlocks fan funding features (Super Chat, Super Thanks, Memberships) at 500 subscribers + 3,000 watch hours, but full ad monetization requires the standard thresholds above.

AdSense Approval Process

Google AdSense is the payment system through which YouTube sends you your earnings. Here is the approval process:

  1. Apply for AdSense: When you apply for YPP, YouTube prompts you to create or link an AdSense account.
  2. Provide identity verification: You must submit a government-issued ID (passport, driver's license, or national ID card).
  3. Provide address verification: Google mails a physical postcard with a PIN code to your address. You enter this PIN in AdSense to verify your address. This step typically takes 1-3 weeks.
  4. Provide tax information: You must complete a tax interview in AdSense. US persons submit a W-9 form. Non-US persons submit a W-8BEN form.
  5. Wait for approval: Google reviews your application. Approval typically takes 2-4 weeks after you submit all required information.

Your AdSense account must be fully approved before YouTube can pay you any earnings. However, you can start monetizing videos (accumulating earnings) while your AdSense application is pending.

Common Rejection Reasons

YouTube rejects many initial monetization applications. The most common reasons include:

  • Reused content: This is the #1 rejection reason. YouTube does not monetize channels that primarily feature content created by others (compilations, reaction videos without significant commentary, re-uploaded clips). Even if you have permission, YouTube considers this "reused."
  • Repetitive content: Channels with mass-produced, formulaic content where videos are indistinguishable from each other. Examples include auto-generated content, text-to-speech narration over stock footage, or bulk-produced videos with minimal creative input.
  • Community Guidelines strikes: Any active strikes on your channel disqualify you from monetization.
  • Copyright issues: Content that uses copyrighted material without permission or fair use justification.
  • Advertiser-unfriendly content: Videos featuring excessive profanity, violence, adult themes, controversial topics, or dangerous activities may be flagged as unsuitable for advertising.
  • Insufficient original content: Channels where most videos are shorts, livestreams without replay value, or very short clips may not demonstrate enough original content for monetization.

How to Apply Step by Step

  1. Sign in to YouTube Studio
  2. Click Earn in the left menu
  3. Review the YouTube Partner Program terms
  4. Check your progress toward the subscriber and watch hours thresholds
  5. Once you meet the requirements, click Apply
  6. Review and accept the Base Terms
  7. Sign up for or link an existing AdSense account
  8. Wait for YouTube's review (typically within 1 month)
  9. If approved, start monetizing your videos by enabling ads

If you are rejected, YouTube will tell you the reason. You can reapply after 30 days if you have addressed the issues.

Revenue Share Model

YouTube's revenue share determines how much money you keep versus how much YouTube takes:

Revenue Source Creator Share YouTube Share
Long-form video ads 55% 45%
YouTube Shorts ads 45% 55%
Channel memberships 70% 30%
Super Chat & Super Stickers 70% 30%
Super Thanks 70% 30%

For long-form videos, if an advertiser pays a $10 CPM, the creator's share of ad revenue is $5.50 (55% of $10). However, the creator's actual RPM is typically lower because not every view generates ad revenue (due to ad blockers, YouTube Premium viewers, skipped ads, etc.).

Shorts Revenue: YouTube Shorts use a different model. Ad revenue from the Shorts feed is pooled together, and creators receive 45% of their allocated share based on their proportion of total Shorts views. This results in much lower per-view earnings compared to long-form content.

Content Policies Affecting Monetization

YouTube enforces several content policies that directly impact your ability to monetize:

  • Advertiser-Friendly Content Guidelines: Content must be suitable for most advertisers. Videos with excessive profanity (especially in the first 7 minutes), graphic violence, adult content, or controversial topics may receive limited or no ads.
  • Yellow icon (limited ads): Videos that partially comply with guidelines may receive a yellow dollar sign, meaning they get fewer and lower-paying ads.
  • Green icon (full monetization): Videos that fully comply receive the green dollar sign and earn maximum ad revenue.
  • Copyright claims: If a video contains copyrighted content (music, clips), the copyright holder may claim the revenue instead of the creator.

Tips for Faster Approval

  1. Create 100% original content: Film yourself, use your own voice, create your own graphics. Avoid reused or repurposed content.
  2. Build a consistent upload schedule: Channels that upload regularly (at least 1-2x per week) demonstrate commitment and grow faster.
  3. Focus on watch time: Create engaging content that keeps viewers watching. Longer watch time helps you reach the 4,000-hour threshold faster.
  4. Avoid controversial topics early on: Build your channel with advertiser-friendly content first. You can expand into edgier topics after monetization is established.
  5. Review YouTube's policies before applying: Make sure every video on your channel complies with monetization policies. Remove any videos that might trigger a rejection.
  6. Write original descriptions and titles: Avoid clickbait or misleading metadata, which can flag your channel for review.
  7. Engage with your community: Respond to comments, use community posts, and build a genuine audience. YouTube favors channels with real engagement.

What Happens After Approval

Once approved for YPP:

  • You can enable monetization on all existing and new videos
  • You gain access to additional revenue features (Memberships, Super Chat, Shopping)
  • Earnings accumulate in your AdSense account
  • YouTube pays you monthly (between the 21st-26th) once you reach the $100 payment threshold
  • You get access to YouTube Analytics revenue reports
  • You can access YouTube's Creator Support team for monetization issues

Your first payment typically arrives 2-3 months after approval, because payments are made on a net-30 basis (you earn in January, get paid in late March).

Conclusion

YouTube monetization through the Partner Program is accessible to any creator who meets the subscriber and watch time thresholds, creates original content, and follows YouTube's policies. The key to approval is demonstrating that your channel adds original value and attracts a genuine audience. Once approved, focus on creating content in high-CPM niches, optimizing for watch time, and diversifying your revenue streams beyond ads to maximize your YouTube income.

How Sponsored Post Pricing Works

Sponsored content is one of the most lucrative monetization methods for bloggers, website owners, and social media creators. Unlike AdSense, which pays pennies per click, a single sponsored post can earn hundreds or even thousands of dollars. However, pricing sponsored content fairly is both an art and a science, and many creators struggle to determine what to charge.

The price of a sponsored post is primarily determined by four factors: your website traffic, your domain authority, your content niche, and the type of content being sponsored. Each of these factors contributes to the value you deliver to the sponsoring brand.

Key Factors in Sponsored Post Pricing

Traffic Volume

Monthly page views are the most fundamental pricing factor. More traffic means more eyes on the sponsored content, which translates to more value for the brand. A common baseline formula uses a rate of $0.01 to $0.05 per page view, depending on other factors. For example, a site with 50,000 monthly page views might charge $500-$2,500 per sponsored post.

Domain Authority (DA)

Domain Authority, developed by Moz, is a score from 1 to 100 that predicts how well a website will rank on search engines. Higher DA means the sponsored content is more likely to rank well and generate long-term organic traffic for the brand. Sites with DA 60+ can typically charge 2-3x more than sites with DA 20-30, even with similar traffic volumes.

Content Niche

The niche you write about significantly affects pricing. Finance, business, legal, and B2B niches command premium rates because the brands in these spaces have higher marketing budgets and higher customer lifetime values. A sponsored post on a finance blog might earn 2-3x what the same post would earn on a gaming or entertainment blog.

Content Type

Not all sponsored content is created equal. A dedicated product review requires more effort and carries more weight than a brief social media mention. Here is how different content types typically compare in pricing:

  • Blog Post (baseline): Standard sponsored article integrated into your content flow.
  • Dedicated Review (1.3x): In-depth product review with your honest assessment.
  • Video Review (1.5x): Video content typically commands premium rates due to higher production effort and engagement.
  • Newsletter Feature (0.85x): Inclusion in your email newsletter. Lower than blog posts because the content has a shorter lifespan.
  • Social Media Post (0.7x): A standalone social media post promoting the brand. Typically the lowest-priced option.
Key Insight: When negotiating sponsored content deals, always consider the total value you provide, not just traffic. A site with 10,000 monthly visitors and DA 70 in the finance niche may be worth more to a brand than a site with 50,000 visitors and DA 25 in general lifestyle.

Negotiation Tips for Sponsored Content

  • Know your metrics: Before entering any negotiation, have your traffic data, DA, engagement rates, and audience demographics ready in a media kit.
  • Start high: Initial quotes should be 20-30% above your minimum acceptable rate to leave room for negotiation.
  • Offer packages: Bundle multiple deliverables (blog post + social media + newsletter) at a slight discount to increase total deal value.
  • Charge for exclusivity: If a brand requests that you not work with competitors, add a 25-50% premium.
  • Include revisions: Specify how many revisions are included in the price. Additional revisions should be billed separately.
  • Get it in writing: Always use a contract that specifies deliverables, timelines, usage rights, and payment terms.

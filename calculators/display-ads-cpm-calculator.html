What Are Display Ads?

Display advertising refers to visual ad formats shown on websites, apps, and digital platforms. Unlike search ads that appear in response to user queries, display ads are shown based on audience targeting, contextual relevance, or retargeting criteria. Display advertising includes banner ads, native ads, rich media ads, video ads, and interstitial ads served through networks like the Google Display Network, programmatic exchanges, and direct publisher deals.

CPM = (Total Ad Spend / Total Impressions) × 1,000

Programmatic vs. Direct Buy

Display ads can be purchased through two primary channels, each with different pricing dynamics:

Programmatic Buying

Programmatic advertising uses real-time bidding (RTB) to purchase display impressions across ad exchanges. Programmatic CPMs are typically lower than direct buys because the inventory is commoditized and competition drives prices down. Average programmatic display CPMs range from $0.50 to $5.00 for standard formats. The advantage of programmatic is scale and efficiency; the disadvantage is less control over exact placement.

Direct Buying

Direct buys involve negotiating directly with publishers for guaranteed ad placements. These typically command premium CPMs ($3-$20+) but offer guaranteed placement on specific pages, preferred positions, and brand safety controls. Direct buys are ideal for brands that need premium visibility on specific high-traffic websites.

Viewability and Its Impact on CPM

Viewability is a critical factor in display advertising pricing. An ad impression is considered "viewable" when at least 50% of the ad's pixels are visible on screen for at least 1 second (for banners) or 2 seconds (for video). Industry average viewability rates are approximately 50-65% for display ads.

Many advertisers now use viewable CPM (vCPM) models, where they only pay for impressions that are actually viewable. vCPM rates are typically 30-50% higher than standard CPM rates, but they ensure that the advertiser's budget is not wasted on unseen impressions.

Ad Placement Best Practices

  • Above the fold: Ads placed above the fold (visible without scrolling) have 66% higher viewability rates and command premium CPMs.
  • In-content placement: Ads embedded within editorial content generate higher engagement than sidebar or footer ads.
  • Sticky/anchor ads: Ads that remain fixed as users scroll have near-100% viewability rates.
  • Avoid ad clutter: Pages with too many ads suffer from banner blindness, reducing engagement for all ads on the page.
  • Mobile optimization: Over 60% of display ad impressions are served on mobile devices. Ensure your creatives are mobile-responsive.

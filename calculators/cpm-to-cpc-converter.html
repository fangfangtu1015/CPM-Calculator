What Is the Relationship Between CPM and CPC?

CPM (Cost Per Mille) and CPC (Cost Per Click) are two sides of the same advertising coin. CPM measures what you pay per 1,000 impressions, while CPC measures what you pay per individual click. The bridge between them is CTR (Click-Through Rate), which tells you what percentage of impressions result in clicks.

Understanding how to convert between CPM and CPC is essential for comparing advertising costs across different platforms and campaigns, especially when some platforms bill on a CPM basis while others use CPC.

CPC = CPM / (CTR × 10)

Where CTR is expressed as a percentage

Example: CPC = $10 / (2 × 10) = $0.50

When to Use CPM vs. CPC

Use CPM When:

  • Brand awareness is your goal: If you want maximum visibility and impressions, CPM campaigns ensure your ad is seen by as many people as possible.
  • Running display/video ads: Banner ads, video ads, and native ads are typically sold on a CPM basis.
  • Targeting broad audiences: For reach-focused campaigns where clicks are not the primary metric.
  • Retargeting campaigns: CPM-based retargeting can be very cost-effective for re-engaging past visitors.

Use CPC When:

  • Direct response is your goal: If you need clicks, conversions, or traffic to your website, CPC ensures you only pay for engaged users.
  • Running search ads: Google Ads search campaigns are primarily CPC-based.
  • Budget control is critical: CPC gives you more predictable costs per action, making budgeting easier.
  • Testing ad creative: CPC campaigns provide clearer data on which creatives drive engagement.

How to Improve Your CTR (and Lower Your CPC)

  • Write compelling ad copy: Clear, benefit-driven headlines with strong calls-to-action significantly improve CTR.
  • Use relevant keywords: Ensure your ad messaging matches the search intent or audience interests.
  • Test ad formats: Video, carousel, and interactive formats typically generate higher CTRs than static images.
  • Optimize landing pages: A seamless experience from ad to landing page improves engagement and reduces bounce rates.
  • Use ad extensions: On Google Ads, sitelinks, callouts, and structured snippets increase ad real estate and CTR.
  • A/B test continuously: Test different headlines, images, and CTAs to find the highest-performing combination.

What Is TikTok CPM?

TikTok CPM (Cost Per Mille) is the amount an advertiser pays for every 1,000 impressions their ad receives on the TikTok platform. As TikTok has grown into one of the world's most popular social media platforms with over 1.5 billion monthly active users, its advertising ecosystem has become increasingly sophisticated, making CPM a critical metric for campaign planning and optimization.

The formula for calculating TikTok CPM is the same as any other platform:

CPM = (Total Ad Spend / Total Impressions) × 1,000

For example, if you spend $800 on TikTok ads and receive 100,000 impressions, your CPM would be ($800 / 100,000) × 1,000 = $8.00. This falls within the typical TikTok CPM range of $4 to $13 in 2026.

TikTok CPC and Minimum Budget

In addition to CPM, TikTok advertisers should be aware of cost-per-click (CPC) benchmarks. The average TikTok CPC ranges from $0.30 to $1.50, which is generally lower than Facebook and Instagram. TikTok also requires a minimum budget of $50 per campaign and $20 per ad group, making it accessible for small businesses while still serving enterprise-level advertisers.

Key Insight: TikTok's average CPC of $0.30-$1.50 is significantly lower than Facebook ($0.50-$2.00) and Instagram ($0.50-$2.50), making it one of the most cost-effective platforms for click-based campaigns.

Factors That Affect TikTok CPM

Audience Targeting

TikTok's audience is skewing older as the platform matures. While Gen Z remains the core demographic, users aged 25-44 are the fastest-growing segment. Broader targeting typically yields lower CPMs, while highly specific audience segments (e.g., luxury buyers in specific metro areas) will drive CPMs higher.

Ad Format and Placement

In-feed video ads are the most common and affordable format. They appear naturally in the For You Page feed and benefit from TikTok's algorithmic content distribution. TopFeed and TopView placements command premium CPMs because they guarantee prominent placement at the top of the feed, ensuring maximum visibility.

Geography

As with all advertising platforms, geography plays a major role. TikTok CPMs in the United States and Western Europe are higher than in Southeast Asia, Latin America, or other emerging markets. However, TikTok's strength in these emerging markets means advertisers can achieve significant reach at lower costs.

Seasonality

TikTok CPMs spike during major shopping events like Black Friday, Cyber Monday, and the holiday season. TikTok has also become a major player in summer campaigns as brands target younger audiences during school breaks.

Tips to Lower Your TikTok CPM

  • Create native-looking content: Ads that look like organic TikTok content perform better and receive lower CPMs due to higher engagement.
  • Use trending sounds and effects: Leveraging trending audio and visual effects improves relevance and reduces costs.
  • Test multiple ad formats: Start with In-Feed Video for the lowest CPM, then test TopFeed for premium placements.
  • Optimize for mobile: All TikTok ads are mobile-first. Ensure your creative is designed for vertical viewing.
  • Run campaigns during off-peak times: Avoid Q4 if budget is limited; January through March typically offers the lowest CPMs.

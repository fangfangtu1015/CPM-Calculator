What Is Instagram CPM?

Instagram CPM (Cost Per Mille) is the amount an advertiser pays for every 1,000 impressions their ad receives on Instagram. As the platform that became the number one US social ad platform in 2025, Instagram has become an essential channel for brands of all sizes, and understanding your CPM is key to managing your advertising costs effectively.

The formula for calculating Instagram CPM is:

CPM = (Total Ad Spend / Total Impressions) × 1,000

For example, if you spend $600 on Instagram ads and receive 50,000 impressions, your CPM would be ($600 / 50,000) × 1,000 = $12.00. This falls within the typical Instagram CPM range of $10 to $15.

Instagram's Rise as the Top Social Ad Platform

Instagram overtook Facebook as the number one US social media advertising platform in 2025, driven by several key factors. The platform's engagement rate of 0.48-0.50% is significantly higher than Facebook's, and its Reels format has become a major driver of user engagement and monetization. With over 2 billion monthly active users, Instagram offers advertisers an unmatched combination of reach and engagement.

Key Insight: Instagram's engagement rate of 0.48-0.50% is roughly 4x higher than Facebook's, which helps justify its higher CPM. When you factor in engagement quality, Instagram often delivers better cost-per-engagement than cheaper platforms.

Instagram CPM vs. Facebook CPM

Instagram and Facebook are both owned by Meta and share the same advertising platform (Meta Ads Manager), but their CPMs differ significantly. Facebook's average CPM tends to be slightly lower at $8-$15, but Instagram's higher engagement rates often result in better overall campaign performance. The choice between the two should be based on your target audience and campaign goals rather than CPM alone.

Factors That Affect Instagram CPM

  • Ad format: Reels ads currently offer the lowest CPMs as Meta pushes the format to compete with TikTok.
  • Audience targeting: Narrow, highly specific audiences increase CPM due to reduced inventory.
  • Ad quality and relevance: Higher-quality ads with better engagement receive lower CPMs through Meta's relevance score system.
  • Seasonality: Q4 sees CPM increases of 25-40% due to holiday advertising competition.
  • Industry: Fashion, beauty, and DTC brands often see lower CPMs on Instagram due to strong organic alignment with the platform.

Tips for Lowering Your Instagram CPM

  • Leverage Reels: Reels ads currently have the lowest CPMs on the platform as Meta prioritizes Reels content distribution.
  • Improve ad creative: High-quality, visually appealing ads receive better relevance scores, which lowers CPM.
  • Use Advantage+ campaigns: Meta's AI-powered targeting can find cheaper impressions by expanding beyond your manual targeting.
  • Test broader audiences: Counterintuitively, broader audiences often produce lower CPMs because Meta's algorithm has more flexibility.
  • Schedule strategically: Running ads during off-peak hours (early morning, weekends) can reduce CPM.
  • Optimize for engagement: Ads that generate likes, comments, and shares signal quality to Meta's algorithm, reducing future CPMs.

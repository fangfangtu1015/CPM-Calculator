What Is TikTok RPM?

TikTok RPM (Revenue Per Mille) measures how much a TikTok creator earns per 1,000 video views from the platform's creator monetization programs. Unlike YouTube, where ad revenue sharing is the primary income source, TikTok offers multiple monetization paths including the Creator Fund, the Creativity Program (formerly Creator Fund beta), TikTok Shop affiliate commissions, LIVE gifts, and series tips.

RPM = (Total Earnings / Total Views) × 1,000

For example, if you earned $15 from 500,000 views through the Creator Fund, your RPM would be ($15 / 500,000) × 1,000 = $0.03. This is typical for the legacy Creator Fund.

TikTok Creator Fund vs. Creativity Program (2026)

TikTok has evolved its creator monetization significantly. Understanding the difference between programs is essential because RPM varies dramatically between them.

TikTok Creator Fund (Legacy)

The original TikTok Creator Fund pays creators based on a pool of money distributed among eligible creators. The RPM for the Creator Fund is notoriously low, typically ranging from $0.02 to $0.04 per 1,000 views. This means a video with 1 million views might earn only $20-$40 through the Creator Fund.

TikTok Creativity Program (Beta)

The Creativity Program is TikTok's newer, more generous monetization program designed to compete with YouTube. It rewards longer-form content (videos over 1 minute) and offers significantly higher RPMs, typically ranging from $0.20 to $1.00+ per 1,000 qualified views. The program requires videos to be over 1 minute long and focuses on original, high-quality content.

Key Insight: TikTok's Creativity Program RPM ($0.20-$1.00) is still significantly lower than YouTube's average RPM ($2-$15). Factors That Affect TikTok RPM

Video Length

The Creativity Program only monetizes videos over 1 minute long. Longer videos (3-10 minutes) tend to generate higher RPMs because they can include more ad breaks and keep viewers engaged longer. The sweet spot appears to be 3-5 minutes for most niches.

Engagement Rate

TikTok's algorithm rewards content that generates high engagement (likes, comments, shares, watch time). Videos with higher engagement rates tend to receive better distribution and higher RPMs because they keep users on the platform longer.

Audience Geography

As with all platforms, viewer location matters. TikTok RPMs are highest for audiences in the United States, followed by the UK, Canada, and Australia. Views from emerging markets generate lower RPMs.

Content Niche

Finance, business, and educational content tends to generate higher RPMs on TikTok because these topics attract higher-value advertisers. Entertainment and comedy content, while capable of generating massive view counts, typically has lower RPMs.

Tips to Increase Your TikTok RPM

  • Join the Creativity Program: If eligible, switch from the Creator Fund to the Creativity Program for 5-25x higher RPMs.
  • Create longer videos: Videos over 1 minute are required for the Creativity Program, and 3-5 minute videos tend to perform best.
  • Focus on quality over quantity: The Creativity Program rewards original, high-quality content. One excellent 3-minute video outperforms ten low-effort clips.
  • Diversify income streams: Combine Creativity Program earnings with TikTok Shop affiliate commissions, LIVE gifts, and brand deals.
  • Target US/UK audiences: Create content in English that appeals to viewers in high-RPM countries.
  • Post consistently: Regular posting signals to TikTok's algorithm that you are an active creator, potentially improving RPM.
  • Use trending sounds and hashtags: Leveraging trends increases views and can improve your overall RPM through better engagement.
